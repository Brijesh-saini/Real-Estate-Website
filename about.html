Our Journey

They say buying a home is a journey, and now we truly understand why. It began with a simple idea, a "what if," that blossomed into countless hours of scrolling, dreaming, and planning. We walked through dozens of houses, trying to imagine our life within their walls. There were moments of hope, a few "almosts," and times we felt discouraged.

But this journey taught us so much—about patience, about compromise, and most importantly, about what "home" really means to us. It's more than just a structure; it's a feeling. And when we finally walked into this place, we just knew. The search is over, but the real adventure is just beginning. We can't wait to fill these rooms with laughter, love, and life.

“ Success isn’t measured by how high we rise, but by how firmly we build—on values, vision, and trust. With purpose and perseverance, growth follows naturally. ”

Mr. Sumer Singh Saini

Chairman and Managing Director

Mr. Sumer Singh Saini is the guiding force of the organization, leading with a clear vision, strong values, and a strategic mindset. With deep commitment and known for his time-bound approach and solution-oriented thinking, he drives innovation across systems, operations, and people—ensuring continuous growth and excellence. Coming from a large family of eight siblings, Mr. Saini's early life was one of resilience and hard work. He initially began as a vegetable seller before becoming a government school teacher in Kotputli, Rajasthan. In 2007, driven by his entrepreneurial spirit, he left teaching to pursue a career in sales and marketing, where he worked until 2011. Following six months of dedicated research in real estate, he chose to embark on a new path in this industry.

Mr. Saini's journey in the real estate sector began in 2012 when he founded Gokul Kripa, along with his three friends. Their venture was a success, and on June 5, 2021–World Environment Day–Mr. Saini launched Riyasat Group, a new venture with the goal of bringing sustainable and economically planned townships with world-class infrastructure and amenities. In 2023, Mr. Saini expanded Riyasat Group's reach from Jaipur to Navi Mumbai, marking the company's next phase of growth. With 14 years of dedicated experience in real estate, Mr. Saini continues to shape the future of integrated township development through innovation, sustainability, and a sharp focus on quality.
